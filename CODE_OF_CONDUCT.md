# Code of Conduct

SlipSurfaceIQ LE adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies to the repository, its issue tracker, pull requests,
discussions and any other project space, and to anyone representing the project in public.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome newcomers and people
of every background and level of experience, and focus criticism on the work, not the
person. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing other
people's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, write to
**slipsurface@proton.me**. Reports are read by the maintainer only and are handled
confidentially. If the report concerns the maintainer, say so in the message and it will
be handled with that in mind; you may also contact GitHub through its
[reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

## Enforcement

The maintainer will review every report and may respond with a correction, a warning, a
temporary ban or a permanent ban, following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
of the Contributor Covenant.
